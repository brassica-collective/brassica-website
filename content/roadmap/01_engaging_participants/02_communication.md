## What success looks like

We need communication channels for:

- Announcing project updates to each other
- General chatter
- Forming and discussing project goals
- Making decisions together
- Tracking tasks within each of our working groups (crews)
- Documenting our processes

## Current status

- We use Matrix for project announcements and general chatter
- We use Loomio for forming and discussing project goals and making decisions together
- We use Tiaga for task management within Crews
- We use our Handbook to document agreed processes
- We are in the process of forming an internal documentation process
