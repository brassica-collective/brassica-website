To achieve this, we meet regularly and collaborate on designing the next steps of the project

## What success looks like

- Participants are gathering regularly
- Participants are contributing to decision-making processes
- Participants are forming Crews

## Current status

- We currently have between 8 and 12 participants each interval
- We are still making decisions with our 'interim processes' and intend to update these once we have tested it out with a few more decisions
- Around 4 to 8 participants regularly opt-in to additional crew responsibilities
