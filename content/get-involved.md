If what we're doing at the Brassica Collective feels like a good fit for you, there are lots of ways to get involved.

## Involvement that fits our capacity

The work needed to be done to make change in the face of the housing crisis and the climate crisis is massive. Some of us envisage that we'll live in Brassica Collective houses, which means we can take the resources that we might normally use to ensure our own housing, and deploy them on this project.

But that isn't the only way to join in. Here are three options:

## 1. Have a discussion

If you'd like to have a further discussion with someone participating in the project, you can drop us a line.

{{< section-link "https://opnform.com/forms/brassica-collective-eoi-kixqst" "Discussion request" >}}

## 2. Express interest in Collaborating

If you'd like to contribute to the project in intermittent ways, and be kept up to date, you might want to become a **Collaborator**.

You can read about what this involves in our [Guidelines for Collaborators](/handbook/howtos/guidelines_collaborators/). If this feels like you, use the following form to express your interest and we'll be in touch.

{{< section-link "https://opnform.com/forms/brassica-collective-eoi-jwed5j" "Collaborator interest" >}}

## 3. Express interest in Participating

If you'd like to be an active participant in the project, taking an active role most months in co-creating what this project becomes, and perhaps using the project to explore your personal relationship to collective housing and how meet your housing needs in these changing times, you may want to become a **Participant**.

You can read about what this involves in the [Participation](/handbook/participation/) section of our handbook. If this feels like you, use the following form to express your interest and we'll be in touch if and when existing participants have capacity.

{{< section-link "https://opnform.com/forms/brassica-collaborator-eoi-i6vohy" "Participant interest" >}}
